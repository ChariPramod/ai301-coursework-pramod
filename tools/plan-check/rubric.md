# Is the plan ready to post?

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grounded diagnosis | Issue, thread, repro observations and candidate diagnosis; Diagnosis and grounding | The proposed cause fits the observed trigger, controls, and failure location. It explains the reported behavior without contradicting the repro or ignoring a discriminating observation. A diagnosis is a working explanation, not a completed root-cause proof: accept a plausible mechanism supported by the trigger and controls, with a concrete way to verify it during implementation. It need not be labelled “hypothesis.” Distinguish a contradictory observation from an unproven implementation detail; only the former, or a missing causal connection, blocks readiness. | required |
| Bounded repair | Diagnosis, approach, files and non-goals; Scope | Changes address the supported cause and stay within the issue. Every proposed change has a necessary role; unrelated cleanup, redesign, feature additions, or sweeping format changes are excluded or explicitly deferred. A short plan can establish limits through precise edits without a separate non-goals heading. | required |
| Buildable approach | Candidate approach and file/module targets compared with supplied source facts; Executability | A contributor can start from the named file, function, component, or other unambiguous location and follow concrete changes that can produce the intended behavior. No central design choice, missing dependency, or unexplained operation is left as “fix it” or “handle edge cases.” A named subsystem and operation plus a bounded tracing step can locate the exact function during implementation. Accept unresolved navigation details when the intended behavior, operation and safety boundary are already specified. No patch, exact function name or line numbers are required. | required |
| Discriminating test | Repro input/output, proposed validation and expected result; Test plan | The plan re-runs the failing scenario or an equivalent regression with its decisive trigger and states an observable result that distinguishes broken from fixed. Tests exercise the changed path rather than bypassing it, disabling the assertion, or merely checking installation/build success. Nearby compatibility checks are expected when the planned change directly threatens existing behavior, not as a universal demand for a large suite. | required |
| Honest claims | Candidate assertions and risks against repro, thread and repo facts; Honesty | The plan does not materially misrepresent completed work, test results, coverage, maintainer authorization or a known limitation. Read diagnosis prose as a proposed causal explanation, using the grounding check to assess its support; do not require every inference to carry a disclaimer or an independent trace. Compare claims with the whole supplied issue/repro record, including its stated regression window. Consequential unknowns have a concrete investigation step. Minor attribution or wording imprecision is advisory when correcting it would leave the supported repair and applicable permission unchanged; invented approval, contradicted test results or concealed blockers still fail. | required |
| Thread and contribution fit | Candidate plan comment and plan against thread highlights and explicit repository requirements; Comms | Responds to applicable maintainer direction and follows explicit contribution, testing, reporting and AI-disclosure rules. Communicates a specific independent plan without blame or unsupported completion claims. Under a policy requiring all AI assistance to be disclosed, state the tool and extent or explicitly no AI use; silence is unclear. Do not invent requirements or demand a fixed comment template when none is prescribed. | required |
| Precise attribution | Plan/comment wording compared with the supplied thread and repro | Names the right source for a suggestion and separates personally tested versions from the reported regression window. Flag minor imprecision here; a material misrepresentation also fails Honest claims. | preferred |
| Easy to review | Plan/comment as a whole | A reader can connect cause, change and verification without searching unrelated material. Concision and equivalent organization count. | preferred |

Ready means accept: every required check must pass. Any required fail or unclear means hold/reject. Preferred checks never alter the verdict. Unclear is reserved for missing or unverifiable evidence needed by a check, not a short plan or an absent optional heading. Grade all rows; identify the smallest missing evidence or correction for each non-pass. Do not grade against a secret checklist. In live mode report voice-guide violations separately; they change the verdict only when they also violate a required check above.
